# Delete Your Account — Fajr Calendar for Android

_Last updated: 13 September 2026_

_This page applies to the **Fajr Calendar: Prayers Sync** app for **Android**, published on Google Play by **Blink22**._

This page explains how to delete your Fajr Calendar account and the data associated with it, whether or not you still have the app installed. For full details on the data the app collects, see the [Android Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md).

## Option 1 — Delete from inside the app

1. Open **Fajr Calendar** and go to the **Profile** tab.
2. Tap **Delete Account**.
3. Review the summary of what will be removed and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

## Option 2 — Request deletion without the app

If you have uninstalled the app, or cannot sign in, send an email to:

**developer@blink22.com**

- Use the subject line **"Delete my Fajr Calendar account"**.
- Send it from the Google or Microsoft email address you used to sign in, so that we can verify the request belongs to you.

We will delete your account and confirm by reply within **30 days** of receiving the request.

You can also reach us through our support page: **https://fajrcalendar.com/support**

## What is deleted

When your account is deleted, we remove from our servers:

- Your profile — name, email address, and account identifier.
- Your saved location, calculation method, madhab, and all prayer-time and sync settings.
- The OAuth tokens that connected your Google or Microsoft calendar and contacts to the app. Access is revoked.
- Your subscription record on our servers.
- Prayer events that the app created in your connected calendar.
- The encrypted event cache stored on your device (cleared by the in-app flow).

Crash reports previously sent to Firebase Crashlytics contain only a pseudonymous numeric account identifier, never your name or email address. After deletion that identifier no longer maps to any account, and the reports expire under Firebase's standard retention period.

## What is kept

- **Events you created manually** in your Google or Outlook calendar are yours and are not touched.
- **Your Google Play subscription** is managed by Google, not by us. Deleting your account does **not** cancel it. Cancel it in the Google Play app under **Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions** to stop future charges.
- Records we are legally required to retain, if any, are kept only for as long as the law requires.

No other data is retained after deletion. Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
